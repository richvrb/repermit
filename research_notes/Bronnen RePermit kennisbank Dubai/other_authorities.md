# Building-modification procedures of Dubai authorities other than Dubai Municipality

Status as of 2026-11-01 (all retrievals on this date). Evidence labels used throughout:
- **[PRIMARY-READ]**: authority document, text actually read (fetched page, or PDF extracted/viewed locally).
- **[PRIMARY-SNIPPET]**: authority document, seen only through a search-result summary; the document itself was not read.
- **[LEAD]**: secondary source (consultant, blog, Scribd). Not evidence.
- **[NOT VERIFIED]**: could not be confirmed.

Nothing behind a login was fetched. Zoning e-Services, Trakhees Online, the DMCC Member Portal, Dubai Trade and Masaar were not opened, so requirements visible only inside those portals are unknown.

## DDA: is there an issue newer than December 2021, what regulations exist, and does it still run under that name and those services?

### Takeaway
DDA still operates under that name and still runs the Building Modification services, now as individual web service pages. No issue of the ID Cards newer than Issue 6 (December 2021) was found, but the web pages have already moved on from it, so they, the current forms and the circulars should be treated as the current layer. DDA publishes no building or zoning regulation of its own on its Laws page; design rules come from the DBC and fire rules from the UAE Fire Code, supported by circulars.

### Cited Findings
**Name and mandate**
- DCCA was renamed Dubai Development Authority and "will remain as the master planning and building services authority within its jurisdiction" — [Circular 308, 6 Jan 2019](https://dda.gov.ae/-/media/3F1445A610484757A99D5A39E4382343.pdf) [PRIMARY-READ]. The current site carries the 2026 footer — [DDA Terms](https://dda.gov.ae/en/terms-and-conditions) [PRIMARY-READ].

**ID Cards version**
- The cover of the file reads Issue No. 6, December 2021, with services M-01 to M-04 and C-01 to C-13 — [ID Cards](https://webzoning.dda.gov.ae/Zoning/Content/dda/ServicesIDs/Building%20Modification%20Services'%20ID%20Cards.pdf) [PRIMARY-READ]. A `?ver=4` URL for the same file is also indexed with the Issue 6 cover text — [ID Cards ver=4](https://webzoning.dda.gov.ae/Zoning/Content/dda/ServicesIDs/Building%20Modification%20Services'%20ID%20Cards.pdf?ver=4) [PRIMARY-SNIPPET]. A sister file exists for fit-out — [Fit-out ID Cards ver=2022](https://webzoning.dda.gov.ae/Zoning/Content/dda/ServicesIDs/Fit-out%20Services'%20ID%20Cards.pdf?ver=2022) [PRIMARY-SNIPPET].

**Current services (web pages)**
- **Building Modification - Design (card M-02):** for completed projects only, after the completion certificate; 5 working days; AED 1,500 plus AED 20 per transaction. Documents: application form (service centre only); approved stamped and proposed drawings per Circular 400 with architectural, structural and mechanical templates; owner NOC; consultant appointment letter; structural calculations and models in soft copy (if applicable); Dubai Civil Defense NOC (if applicable). Approved design valid 6 months — [Building Modification - Design](https://dda.gov.ae/en/planning-development/design/building-modification-design) [PRIMARY-READ].
- **Building Modification - Permit (card M-03):** requester contractor; online only; 2 working days; AED 1.0/ft² of additional or relevant built-up area; renewal AED 200 per 3 months up to 1 year. Documents: application (service centre only); appointment letter signed by owner, consultant and contractor; site mobilization layout (if applicable) — [Building Modification Permit](https://dda.gov.ae/en/planning-development/construction/permits-nocs/building-modification-permit) [PRIMARY-READ].
- **Building Modification - Completion (card M-04):** 3 working days; Civil Defense completion certificate (if applicable), owner NOC, compliance certificate from the main consultant; all DDA comments cleared; valid site plan needed — [Completion](https://dda.gov.ae/en/planning-development/construction/inspections-completions/building-modification-completion) [PRIMARY-READ].
- **Design Revision:** covers Building Modification among other scopes (compare card C-06); 5 working days; AED 1,000 + AED 1.0/ft² for additional modified area if approved; structural calculations and model for structural revisions only; relevant local authority NOC if applicable — [Design Revision](https://dda.gov.ae/planning-development/design/design-revision) [PRIMARY-READ].
- **Fit-Out Permit:** 2 working days; AED 0.90/ft² (min AED 200, max AED 10,000); needs Ejari, owner NOC, authority NOCs per a matrix, drawings per Circular 400; valid 6 months. Added structural elements, change of use or added GFA require a separate Building Modification request, and fit-out fees are not refunded if DDA reclassifies — [Fit-Out Permit](https://dda.gov.ae/planning-development/construction/permits-nocs/fit-out-permit) [PRIMARY-READ].
- **Form:** the application form is "ZA-DC-F-69 Issue 10", marked Public and undated. It still offers three submittal types: Design Review and Permit, Design Review, and Permit. It lists the documents for each — [ZA-DC-F-69A](https://dda.gov.ae/-/media/Project/TECOM/Media/DDA/General/DDA_PDF/PD_PDF/03-Construction/Permits-and-NOCs/M-03-BUILDING-MODIFICATION-PERMIT/ZA-DC-F-69A-Building-Modification-Design-Approval-Request.pdf) [PRIMARY-READ].

**Differences between the 2021 cards and the current sources**
- M-03 fee: the card says "NA"; the web page says AED 1.0/ft². The card puts the per-ft² charge in M-01 — [ID Cards](https://webzoning.dda.gov.ae/Zoning/Content/dda/ServicesIDs/Building%20Modification%20Services'%20ID%20Cards.pdf); [Permit page](https://dda.gov.ae/en/planning-development/construction/permits-nocs/building-modification-permit) [PRIMARY-READ].
- M-03 appointment letter: the form says contractor appointment letter from owner/tenant; the web page says a letter signed by owner, consultant and contractor — [ZA-DC-F-69A](https://dda.gov.ae/-/media/Project/TECOM/Media/DDA/General/DDA_PDF/PD_PDF/03-Construction/Permits-and-NOCs/M-03-BUILDING-MODIFICATION-PERMIT/ZA-DC-F-69A-Building-Modification-Design-Approval-Request.pdf); [Permit page](https://dda.gov.ae/en/planning-development/construction/permits-nocs/building-modification-permit) [PRIMARY-READ].
- M-03 channel: the card lists a customer service route; the web page lists online only — same sources [PRIMARY-READ].

**Circulars relevant to modifications**
- [Circular 400, 29 Sep 2021](https://dda.gov.ae/-/media/Project/TECOM/Media/DDA/General/DDA_PDF/PD_PDF/02-Design/C-06-DESIGN-REVISION/Circular-400.pdf) [PRIMARY-READ]: A1 drawings in DWF, one sheet per file, monochrome, discipline tabs. For modifications, the approved drawings are clouded yellow and named "As Approved", the proposed drawings clouded red and named "As Proposed". This is directly usable as a check rule.
- [Circular 338, 11 Dec 2019](https://dda.gov.ae/-/media/403EAC241E074C8DB6385C46DE4896E9.pdf) [PRIMARY-READ]: no Fit-out Permit for units of 100 m² or less or mall units, but Building Modification is still required for structural changes, change of use, added mezzanine, and adding, omitting, merging or separating units or areas.
- [Circular 318, 5 May 2019](https://dda.gov.ae/-/media/116360B0DDBF4E1E89473C90A530CEAD.pdf) [PRIMARY-READ]: lists works needing no Fit-out Permit, such as painting, non-structural demolition inside the unit, and false ceiling replacement without affecting fire systems. Whether 318 and 338 are still in force was [NOT VERIFIED].
- [Circular 426, 13 Dec 2022](https://dda.gov.ae/-/media/46AC6B52AF224608B0C7A035C4F7B5A8.pdf) [PRIMARY-READ]: Building Modification Permit valid one year; renewal required from 2 Jan 2023 until the project is closed.
- [Circular 409, 9 Mar 2022](https://dda.gov.ae/-/media/9B627B3F7C9E45F3990583BEC81D1B87.pdf) [PRIMARY-READ]: designs submitted must comply with the DBC as of January 2022.
- [Circular 667, 19 May 2026](https://dda.gov.ae/-/media/4B489270690D4C8293B5642765469D2B.pdf) [PRIMARY-READ]: the UAE Fire and Life Safety Code of Practice is mandatory across construction, modification, alteration and demolition. Newer restatement of [Circular 391, 21 May 2021](https://dda.gov.ae/-/media/1241A746AD574589AE285F1D24A79881.pdf) [PRIMARY-READ].
- Newest planning circulars listed: 686 (14 Oct 2026, site visit system), 680 (10 Sep 2026, signboards and hoarding), 656 (16 Apr 2026, scaffolding), 645 (17 Dec 2025, soil report) — [Circulars page](https://dda.gov.ae/en/legal-database/circulars-and-announcements) [PRIMARY-READ, titles only].

**Regulations**
- The Laws page lists no development control, building, fire or zoning document; it covers companies, licensing, employment, media and AML, plus the Creative Clusters law — [Laws and Regulations](https://dda.gov.ae/en/legal-database/laws-and-regulations) [PRIMARY-READ].
- DDA's DBC briefing says the DBC replaces regulations on building design published by DM, DDA, Trakhees and DSOA, but planning development regulations by Developers or Planning Authorities are not replaced; buildings follow limits in development control regulations and/or affection plans — [Objectives of DBC](https://dda.gov.ae/-/media/B6D27743B4FC484C80BF40509A6F3D55.pdf) [PRIMARY-READ].
- Related but unread: [Master Planning Guidelines Issue 2](https://dda.gov.ae/-/media/Project/TECOM/Media/DDA/Planning-and-development/Master-Planning-Services/pdf/Master-Planning-Guidelines-download.pdf) [PRIMARY-SNIPPET]; the [Development Information System](https://gis.dda.gov.ae/DIS/) is linked from service pages (not opened).
- Under the Creative Clusters law, establishments are reportedly outside DM rules except for health, public safety, food control and environment — [Law 15/2014 EN](https://dda.gov.ae/-/media/BAE2F2CC37134C81BEF31967C4E00193.pdf) [PRIMARY-SNIPPET].

### Inferences
- For the DDA layer of the knowledge base, use the web service pages, Form ZA-DC-F-69 and Circulars 338, 400, 409, 426 and 667. Keep Issue 6 only as a historical overview and for the map of optional services.
- Zoning limits appear to be plot-specific (site plan, DCR, affection plan) and not a single DDA document, so RePermit would need them per plot as input.

### Gaps
- No proof that Issue 6 is the latest: DDA gives no version register for these cards. **[NOT VERIFIED]**
- Whether a separate web page for M-01 (Design and Permit) still exists was not checked; the form still offers it.
- Templates ZADC-T-120/121/122 (modification checklists per discipline) were found but **not read**. They are probably the most useful documents for assessment rules, so they should be fetched next. The same applies to ZA-DC-F-69B and the Fit-out NOC matrix.
- The full circular list was filtered by the fetch tool, so there may be more relevant circulars.
- Full list of DDA zones not established. TECOM's page names several business districts — [DDA freezone page](https://dda.gov.ae/en/freezone) [PRIMARY-SNIPPET].

## Trakhees (PCFC) and JAFZA: who is competent, what guides exist, are they retrievable, and what do they require?

### Takeaway
Trakhees CED, Modification & Compliance section, is the explicit authority for modifications to completed buildings in its jurisdiction, and it has the best-structured public procedure set of all authorities reviewed. JAFZA runs a separate landlord-type NOC on top of the permit.

### Cited Findings
**Trakhees**
- Any modification or fit-out in a completed facility with a Building Completion Certificate (BCC) needs a Modification Permit before works start. CED's Modification Compliance section acts as 'Authority'. Revisions to ongoing projects with a current Building Permit are outside this procedure — [PCFC-TRK-CED-MF-CP-01, Rev. 02, Sept 2024, Public](https://pcfc.ae/en/Service%20Documents/PCFC-TRK-CED-MF-CP-01,%20Client%20Procecure%20for%20obtaining%20Modification%20Permits.pdf) [PRIMARY-READ, all 15 pages viewed].
- Five submission types: New, Revalidation (if not started within 6 months), Cancellation, Amendment (change of party), Revision. Other services: Mobilization NOC, Temporary Facility, NOC for additional DEWA. Minor racking and machinery go through CED Facility Permit, with limits of rack height 3.5 m and layout 250 m² (Circular CEDGR-85) — same source.
- Steps: online application with folder codes; helpdesk accepts, rejects or requests information; initial fees; inspection and review by CED; approved or not approved; final payment; download link valid one month. Target times: new 7 WD, revision 10 WD, revalidation 2 WD, cancellation 5 WD — same source.
- If stamped CED drawings are missing, the applicant requests copies through form TRK-CED-DC-CF01a; only if CED has none may the consultant or contractor prepare stamped "As Existing" drawings. This is relevant to the as-built problem in existing buildings — same source.
- Documents per [TRK-CED-MF-CF01a](https://pcfc.ae/en/Service%20Documents/TRK-CED-MF-CF01a,%20Application%20form%20for%20Modification%20NOC%20(Retail,%20Landlord%20Unit,%20Industrial).pdf) [PRIMARY-READ, pages 1-2 of 4 viewed, footer Dec 2023]. Documents folder: client trade licence; lease, Ejari, title deed or Trakhees site plan; DCD NOC; landlord, developer or business unit NOC; Certificate of Conformity (modification works). Drawings folder: key plan, floor plans, elevations, sections and utilities layout (existing and proposed); design calculations and structural drawings if applicable; Master Developer approved drawings; demolition layout with method statement if any; latest CED-approved drawings. Submission online in PDF.
- Scope tick-boxes on the form: boundary wall, full-height partitions, external painting, racking, paving and landscaping, swimming pools, machinery, mezzanine, staircase/ramps, car parking/shade/pergola, others — same source.
- A newer form version (Rev. 05, April 2025) at a different URL is reported by search — [form, www URL](https://www.pcfc.ae/en/Service%20Documents/TRK-CED-MF-CF01a,%20Application%20form%20for%20Modification%20NOC.pdf) [PRIMARY-SNIPPET; fetch returned 403].
- Completion: [PCFC-TRK-CED-MF-CP-02, Rev. 02, Dec 2022](https://pcfc.ae/en/Service%20Documents/PCFC-TRK-CED-MF-CP-02,%20Client%20Procedure%20for%20requesting%20Modification%20Completion%20Certificate%20(MCC)%20Modification%20Cancelation%20Inspection%20(MPC).pdf) [PRIMARY-READ, cover and keyword search only]; [TRK-CED-MF-CF02a, Rev. 02, July 2024](https://pcfc.ae/en/Service%20Documents/TRK-CED-MF-CF02a,%20Application%20form%20for%20MCC.pdf) [PRIMARY-READ] requires the permit with all revisions, structural inspection record, DCD completion certificate, Trakhees final demarcation certificate and stamped drawings.
- Structural inspection: [PCFC-TRK-CED-MF-CP-03, Rev. 02, May 2025](https://pcfc.ae/en/Service%20Documents/PCFC-TRK-CED-MF-CP-03,%20Requesting%20for%20Modification%20Structural%20Inspections.pdf) [PRIMARY-READ, first pages]: no concrete work on structural elements before requesting inspection, under Circular 10 of 2024 (24 Apr 2024).
- Operational rule: modification or design must be in line with DBC 2021 Edition, and the CED permit is mandatory for all establishments — [PCFC-TRK-LSD-CLS-REG02, Rev. 01, March 2023](https://pcfc.ae/en/Rules%20and%20Regulations/Trakhees/Licensing%20Department/Licensing/Rules%20&%20Regulations/PCFC-TRK-LSD-CLS-REG02,%20Operational%20Requirements%20for%20Trakhees.pdf) [PRIMARY-READ].
- Domain change: the old trakhees.ae regulations URL now redirects to pcfc.ae, and a request to the www.pcfc.ae home page returned 403. Direct PDF links on pcfc.ae (without www) worked. Note that [Circular 05-24 on Mobilization and Signboards](https://www.pcfc.ae/en/Rules%20and%20Regulations/Trakhees/Civil%20Engineering%20Department/Modification/Circulars/Circular%2005-24%20Mobilization%20and%20Signboards%20for%20Modification%20Works%20in%20Existing%20Buildings.pdf) also returned 403 [title only, NOT READ].

**JAFZA**
- Jafza issues a Modifications NOC for changes inside and outside the leased unit, via the Dubai Trade portal; 5 working days; no charges. Documents for modifications: Jafza trade licence, a building or modification permit, approved drawings, site photographs. For plot facilities: Trakhees lease drawing or affection plan, proposed architectural layout, CED-approved as-built drawings. Guide dated 3 April 2024 — [Jafza Services Guidebook](https://www.jafza.ae/resources/guides/modifications-no-objection-certificate-request) [PRIMARY-READ].
- An older circular refers to an EHS NOC and a Trakhees CED Building Permit for modifications in the DMC context — [circular 02-12](https://pcfc.ae/en/Rules%20and%20Regulations/Trakhees/Environment,%20Health%20&%20Safety/Ports%20&%20Maritime/Circulars/circular02-12trakheesmandatoryapprovalrequirementsfordmcclients.pdf) [PRIMARY-SNIPPET]. Its current validity is [NOT VERIFIED].

### Inferences
- In Jebel Ali, the approval chain appears to be: Trakhees CED permit first (the Jafza guide treats the permit as an input and refers to CED drawings), then the Jafza NOC. The order is inferred, not stated.
- Trakhees's procedure is the strongest candidate for a second jurisdiction in the demo, because it is public, versioned, English and marked Public. Its document checklist is simple and maps well to DDA's.

### Gaps
- Exact Trakhees jurisdiction boundaries: no official list was retrieved. Consultant pages disagree on whether DWC, Palm Jumeirah, International City and similar areas fall under Trakhees — [lead 1](https://genc-website-ccs.pages.dev/guides/trakhees-vs-dda-vs-dm-approval/), [lead 2](https://mokaab-crafted-visions.lovable.app/guides/dubai-authority-approvals) [LEAD].
- Circulars CEDGR-133 (12 Dec 2021), CEDGR-19 (17 Nov 2015) and CEDGR-85 (14 May 2019), which define works needing approval, were **not read**. Same for "Design Development Regulations – Facilities" and the fee schedule ("as per project specification").
- CF01a Rev. 05 is not read, so the checklist above may be one or two revisions behind.
- CP-02 procedure steps were only keyword-searched.
- EHS's own approval procedure for modifications was not found beyond the older circular.

## DIFC, Dubai South, DMCC, and similar zones: who is competent, what guides exist, are they retrievable, and what do they require?

### Takeaway
None of these publishes a full permit procedure like DDA or Trakhees. DIFC runs an NOC stage that precedes DDA. DMCC acts as master developer. DHCC has a strong 2026 fit-out guideline. No primary Dubai South or DIEZ procedure was found.

### Cited Findings
**DIFC**
- Building Fit-Out Work NOC (code FTW), from the DIFC Property Development Department (PDD). It is required before approaching DDA and before work starts. Fee AED 3,000 + 5% VAT; maximum 5 working days. Documents: DIFC commercial licence, title deed, A3 existing and proposed drawings stamped by building FM/body corporate/owner, approved strata plan, contractor's DDA registration proof and trade licence, NOC request letters from tenant, unit owner and building management. No last-updated date — [Third-Party Buildings](https://www.difc.com/business/third-party-buildings) [PRIMARY-READ].
- A separate NOC gateway covers structural, fit-out, external and signage works: DIFC PDD NOC, building management NOC, RTA NOC, DDA NOC "with guidance from DIFC PDD", and other permits (DM, DCD, DCAA, Dubai Police) where applicable — [Submittals, Requests and External Works NOC](https://www.difc.com/business/submittals-requests-and-external-works-noc) [PRIMARY-READ].
- DIFC's Handbooks & Fees page lists no construction or fit-out guide — [Handbooks & Fees](https://www.difc.com/business/handbook-fees) [PRIMARY-READ].
- A "DIFC Built Environment Design Manual", three work categories and an OFM route appear only in a commercial guide; conflicting names are used (OFM, DIFCA) — [lead](https://uaefreezonefinder.com/uae-difc-construction-noc-guide-2026/) [LEAD, NOT VERIFIED]. No DIFC page confirmed these.

**DMCC (JLT)**
- Regulations [Version 3.0, issued 29 June 2021](https://jlt.ae/hubfs/C5-Migrated-Files/All-Files/DMCC_Master_Community_Regulations_2021_EN.pdf?hsLang=en) [PRIMARY-READ, clause 12]: no Alterations without prior written approval of the Master Developer (12.1); it may issue Construction Guidelines (12.2) and delegate (12.3); it liaises with Relevant Authorities on building permits (12.4). External alterations are the defined term.
- A JLT Retail Tenant Handbook lists tenant approvals including a DM Building Permit and DCD Fit-Out NOC and Completion Certificate, and cites the UAE Fire & Life Safety Code; later alterations need written approval from management — [Handbook R4](https://jlt.ae/hubfs/JLT/Community%20Governance%20web/Retail%20Tenant%20Handbook%20R4.pdf?hsLang=en) [PRIMARY-READ, keyword search only; undated].
- A JLT fit-out NOC form is linked from DMCC's support page — [DMCC Support](https://dmcc.ae/members/support) [PRIMARY-READ]. The form itself was not opened.
- The older, undated [fit-out NOC guideline](https://www.dmcc.ae/application/files/5914/8411/4155/NOC_for_Licenced_Co._Fit_Out.pdf) and the 2020 [Community Regulations](https://www.dmcc.ae/application/files/2415/7658/6091/Master_Community_Regulations.pdf) return 404 [PRIMARY-SNIPPET only]. The Type A/B/C tiers and "Community Management" route come from a commercial guide — [lead](https://uaefreezonefinder.com/uae-dmcc-fit-out-permit-guide-2026/) [LEAD, NOT VERIFIED].

**Dubai Healthcare City (DHCC, a similar zone)**
- [GL-DHCA-ENG-01 Engineering Fit-out Guidelines](https://dhcc.ae/gallery/GL-DHCA-ENG-01_Engineering%20Fit-out%20Guidelines_V1.0.pdf) (V1.0 per filename, effective 2 January 2026, 39 pp.) [PRIMARY-READ in part]: covers General, Modification and Minor fit-out through the Masaar portal. Documents include DCD initial approval and stamped drawings, and "Approved plans (As-Built) / DDA Approved As-Built", landlord NOC, and existing and proposed architectural and MEP drawings. Structural modifications are restricted without explicit DHCA Engineering approval. It must be read with DCD, DEWA, DM, DDA, DHA, MOHAP and Empower regulations.

**Dubai South**
- The site lists "construction permits" among logistics services without details — [Logistics Operations](https://www.dubaisouth.ae/en/work/logistics/operations) [PRIMARY-SNIPPET]. The [planning and development forms page](https://dubaisouth.ae/forms/planning-and-development) and [Laws & Regulation page](https://www.dubaisouth.ae/en/library) show no downloadable document [PRIMARY-READ].
- A Scribd copy titled "Dubai South Development Process Guide" describes 14 stages for new projects; no issuer, date or modification route is visible — [Scribd](https://www.scribd.com/document/730112156/dsdevelopmentcontrolsubmissionsprocess) [LEAD].
- Consultant pages claim Dubai South runs its own approval plus Civil Defence clearance — [lead 1](https://approvaldubai.ae/services/dubai-south-approval/), [lead 2](https://lavaspace.ae/blogs/dubai-municipality-fit-out-approval-process-2) [LEAD]. Another places Dubai World Central under Trakhees — [lead](https://genc-website-ccs.pages.dev/guides/trakhees-vs-dda-vs-dm-approval/) [LEAD]. These conflict.

**DIEZ / Dubai Silicon Oasis**
- No primary procedure found. The DBC briefing names DSOA as a former publisher of building design regulations — [Objectives of DBC](https://dda.gov.ae/-/media/B6D27743B4FC484C80BF40509A6F3D55.pdf) [PRIMARY-READ]. DIEZ appears as an approving body only in vendor brochures [LEAD].

### Inferences
- In DIFC, the DDA appears to be the authority behind the NOC stage: DIFC requires its NOC "before approaching" DDA, and contractors need DDA registration. So DIFC would add an NOC layer on top of DDA procedures. No document states this outright.
- DHCC's reference to DDA as-builts suggests the same pattern there.
- DMCC's clause 12 suggests a developer-approval layer, with permits from a Relevant Authority. The handbook points to DM for JLT retail, but this is one tenant-handbook line.

### Gaps
- Dubai South: competent authority and procedure **unverified**. A direct enquiry (info@dubaisouth.ae) is likely needed.
- DIFC: no technical design standard or fire requirement is published in retrievable form. The [Operating Regulations](https://assets.difc.com/v1/media/edge/images/dubaiintern0078-difcexperie96c5-production-3253/media/project/difcexperiences/difc/difcwebsite/documents/difc_docs/annex-b_operating-regulations.pdf) surfaced in search but were not read.
- DMCC: Construction Guidelines and current fit-out guidelines not found. The building-permit authority for non-retail JLT units is unverified.
- Not reviewed: Dubai Maritime City, Meydan, Nakheel and other master developers, and the DHCA Fit-out Policy & Procedure and Rule No. 2.

## Do these authorities apply the Dubai Building Code and UAE Fire Code, or have their own building regulations?

### Takeaway
Yes for the DBC, by law and by explicit statement from DDA and Trakhees. The UAE Fire Code is confirmed by DDA and is applied in practice through DCD approval elsewhere. Own regulations survive at planning, procedural and developer level, not as competing building design codes.

### Cited Findings
- Decree 45 of 2021, Art. 3: the DBC applies to designs permitted after the effective date throughout the Emirate, including Special Development Zones and free zones such as DIFC; Art. 4 binds public and private entities — [Decree 45/2021](https://dlp.dubai.gov.ae/Legislation%20Reference/2021/Decree%20No.%20(45)%20of%202021%20Concerning%20the%20Dubai%20Building%20Code.html) [PRIMARY-READ].
- **Trakhees:** DBC mandatory for all permit applications from 30 Dec 2021, superseding existing related Trakhees codes — [CEDGR-135, 20 Dec 2021](https://pcfc.ae/en/Rules%20and%20Regulations/Trakhees/Civil%20Engineering%20Department/Permits%20(Facilities)/Circulars/CEDGR-135%20Dubai%20Building%20Code%20Circular%202021.pdf) [PRIMARY-READ]. CP-01 still lists older references beside it, such as BS 8110, UBC 1997, IBC and "Urban Planning & MEP Services Regulations", and DCD — [CP-01 section 8](https://pcfc.ae/en/Service%20Documents/PCFC-TRK-CED-MF-CP-01,%20Client%20Procecure%20for%20obtaining%20Modification%20Permits.pdf) [PRIMARY-READ].
- **DDA:** DBC per Circular 409; UAE Fire Code per Circular 667, both above [PRIMARY-READ].
- **Existing buildings:** DDA's briefing says the DBC applies to new buildings and to changes to existing buildings as described in Table A.1 of the code; projects with a final approved design need not conform; the UAE Fire Code is cross-referenced throughout — [Objectives of DBC](https://dda.gov.ae/-/media/B6D27743B4FC484C80BF40509A6F3D55.pdf) [PRIMARY-READ, undated]. The Decree itself does not address existing buildings directly — [Decree 45/2021](https://dlp.dubai.gov.ae/Legislation%20Reference/2021/Decree%20No.%20(45)%20of%202021%20Concerning%20the%20Dubai%20Building%20Code.html) [PRIMARY-READ].
- **DHCC:** business partners must comply with the UAE Fire and Life Safety Code of Practice and obtain DCD approval; the extracted text does not name the DBC — [GL-DHCA-ENG-01](https://dhcc.ae/gallery/GL-DHCA-ENG-01_Engineering%20Fit-out%20Guidelines_V1.0.pdf) [PRIMARY-READ, keyword search].
- **JLT:** handbook cites the UAE Fire & Life Safety Code and DM building codes — [Handbook R4](https://jlt.ae/hubfs/JLT/Community%20Governance%20web/Retail%20Tenant%20Handbook%20R4.pdf?hsLang=en) [PRIMARY-READ, keyword search].
- **DIFC:** its pages name no DBC or fire code, though they list DCD where applicable; the Decree names DIFC explicitly — [Third-Party Buildings](https://www.difc.com/business/third-party-buildings) [PRIMARY-READ].

### Inferences
- The DBC with the UAE Fire Code can serve as a single technical layer across jurisdictions, with authority-specific procedure layers on top. Authority documents should be tagged per jurisdiction rather than merged.
- Trakhees's old reference list means an automated reading of CP-01 could wrongly cite a superseded code. Add a precedence rule: CEDGR-135 over the list where they overlap in building design. This reading needs a human check.

### Gaps
- DBC Table A.1 itself was not read here, though it decides how far an existing building must be upgraded; it is the crucial clause for RePermit. **[Check against the DBC research notes.]**
- No Dubai South or DMCC statement on DBC adoption was found, other than the Decree's general reach.
- Whether DBC or Fire Code editions after 2021 and 2018 changed these positions was not checked.

## What are the reuse terms of these documents, and what is the source register?

### Takeaway
No authority grants a reuse licence. DDA and Jafza terms expressly restrict copying, and DDA also bars automated scraping and limits use to personal, non-commercial purposes. Ingesting these documents in a product knowledge base should be treated as needing permission or legal review. Verbatim display is the higher risk.

### Cited Findings
- **DDA:** "You may not copy, modify, distribute, sell, or lease any part of the Sites"; automated scraping barred; Sites limited to personal, non-commercial use; rights reserved — [DDA Terms, cl. 2.2, 5.1, 5.2](https://dda.gov.ae/en/terms-and-conditions) [PRIMARY-READ]. Downloaded PDFs are not separately addressed.
- **Jafza:** owner or licensee of all content; no copying, distribution or reuse without written permission; commercial use needs a licence — [Jafza Terms](https://www.jafza.ae/terms-and-conditions) [PRIMARY-READ].
- **PCFC/Trakhees:** terms not retrievable (403). Procedures are marked "Classification: Public", which is not a licence — [CP-01](https://pcfc.ae/en/Service%20Documents/PCFC-TRK-CED-MF-CP-01,%20Client%20Procecure%20for%20obtaining%20Modification%20Permits.pdf) [PRIMARY-READ].
- **DIFC:** terms not found (guessed URL returned 404). **Dubai South:** a [Terms link](https://www.dubaisouth.ae/en/terms-of-use) exists, not read. **DMCC, DHCC:** terms not checked.
- **Decree 45/2021:** page footer credits the Supreme Legislation Committee; no separate licence — [Decree](https://dlp.dubai.gov.ae/Legislation%20Reference/2021/Decree%20No.%20(45)%20of%202021%20Concerning%20the%20Dubai%20Building%20Code.html) [PRIMARY-READ].
