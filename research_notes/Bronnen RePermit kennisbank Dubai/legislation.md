Evidence labels used throughout:

- **[R-direct]**: the official PDF was downloaded and its text or page image was read directly.
- **[R-tool]**: the official page was read through the fetch tool, which returns a machine summary. Article numbers and quotes are as that tool reported them and need a human check before they enter a ruleset.

## Resolution 225/2024: what does it amend in the DBC, and is there a newer edition or amendment?

### Takeaway
It is Administrative Resolution (225) of 2024 of the Director General of Dubai Municipality. It is narrow: it relaxes seven DBC parameters for plots of 250 m² or less and the buildings on them. No newer DBC edition or later amendment was found up to 1 November 2026, but the absence is not proven.

### Cited Findings
- Official title: "قرار إداري رقم (225) لسنة 2024 بشأن تعديل بعض المحددات البنائية في كود دبي للبناء" (amending some building parameters in the DBC). Issued 16 July 2024 by Director General Dawood Al Hajiri. [R-direct] — [PDF](https://dmpmedia.dm.gov.ae/uploads/2024/08/قرار-إداري-رقم-225-لسنة-2004.pdf); [DM page](https://www.dm.gov.ae/documents/administrative-resolution-no-225-of-2024-regarding-amending-some-building-specifications-in-the-dubai-building-code/)
- The file name says "2004"; this is a typo in the upload, because the document itself says 2024. DM's own English lists use two titles: "Administrative Decision ... construction parameters" and "Administrative Resolution ... building specifications". The PDF header carries a "SHARED – CONFIDENTIAL" mark although it is published. [R-direct] — [DM agency list](https://www.dm.gov.ae/municipality-business/buildings-regulation-permits-agency/)
- Scope (art. 1–2): any land in the Emirate of 250 m² or less, and the building on it. [R-direct] — [PDF](https://dmpmedia.dm.gov.ae/uploads/2024/08/قرار-إداري-رقم-225-لسنة-2004.pdf)
- Art. 3: the parameters in the annexed table may be modified as authorised there. The table has seven items: [R-direct] — [PDF](https://dmpmedia.dm.gov.ae/uploads/2024/08/قرار-إداري-رقم-225-لسنة-2004.pdf)
  1. Lifts: exemption if the building is ground + 2 floors or less and has 100 occupants or fewer (both conditions).
  2. Lift lobby on the ground floor: 1.8 m width.
  3. Corridor and lift lobby on typical floors: 1.5 m width.
  4. Shop toilets: one shared unisex toilet per shop of at most 100 m², or pooled toilets per the code.
  5. and 6. Exemption from bulky-waste space and recycling space in the refuse room.
  7. Exemption from the extra 300 mm parking width beside columns and walls, while minimum clear dimensions remain.
- Art. 4: the resolution is attached to the code and read with it. Art. 5: any conflicting text in the code or another administrative resolution is repealed. Art. 6: published on the websites of DM and the competent entity, in force 30 days after publication. [R-direct] — [PDF](https://dmpmedia.dm.gov.ae/uploads/2024/08/قرار-إداري-رقم-225-لسنة-2004.pdf)
- Its preamble names Administrative Resolution 308/2021 as the instrument approving the DBC. That resolution's text was not found. A 2023 resolution cites it as well [S]. — [PDF](https://dmpmedia.dm.gov.ae/uploads/2024/08/قرار-إداري-رقم-225-لسنة-2004.pdf)
- Amendment route: Decree 45/2021 art. 6(b) has DM review the code periodically. The Director General approves amendments, which must be published on the DM and competent-entity websites. Gazette publication is not the route. [R-tool] — [Decree 45/2021](https://dlp.dubai.gov.ae/Legislation%20Reference/2021/Decree%20No.%20(45)%20of%202021%20Concerning%20the%20Dubai%20Building%20Code.html)
- DBC A.9.1 says the code is typically updated on a two- to three-year cycle, with addenda between cycles. A.9.2 says this is the first edition. [R-direct] — [DBC 2021](https://dmpmedia.dm.gov.ae/uploads/2021/12/Dubai%20Building%20Code_English_2021%20Edition_compressed.pdf)
- The DM DBC page, last modified 1 November 2026, names only the 2021 Edition and lists no amendments. [R-tool] — [DM DBC page](https://www.dm.gov.ae/municipality-business/planning-and-construction/dubai-building-code-2/)
- The DM agency legislation list shows 225/2024 as the only item that names the DBC apart from Decree 45/2021. [R-tool] — [DM agency list](https://www.dm.gov.ae/municipality-business/buildings-regulation-permits-agency/)
- Targeted searches of dm.gov.ae, dlp.dubai.gov.ae and mediaoffice.ae for a 2025–2026 DBC amendment returned none. They did return AR 112/2026 (Safety Guide for Construction Works), which is a separate instrument. [S] — [AR 112/2026](https://dlp.dubai.gov.ae/Legislation%20Reference/2026/Administrative%20Resolution%20No.%20(112)%20of%202026%20Approving%20the%20Safety%20Guide%20for%20Construction%20Works.pdf)

### Inferences
- The effective date is about 12 September 2024 if the DM listing date of 13 August 2024 is the publication date. That is an assumption.
- Nothing in 225/2024 limits it to new buildings, so it can apply to changes to a small-plot building, but it says nothing about apartment walls. The source-analysis line "amends parts of the code" overstates its relevance to the 4A/4B case.
- For RAG, the DBC PDF alone is not the current text. 225/2024 needs its own chunk with a plot-area condition (`plot_area_m2 <= 250`), linked to the affected clauses.
- The code's Parts B, D and probably G are the affected ones, but 225/2024 cites no clause numbers. Mapping each item to a clause is still to be done.

### Gaps
- "No newer edition" is an absence-of-evidence finding. The code's own 2–3 year cycle makes a new edition plausible. The DBC page should be rechecked before any release.
- DDA, Trakhees and other competent-entity websites were not checked for addenda, although Decree 45/2021 art. 6(b) also requires publication there.
- The DM circulars page shows no dates. Not every circular was opened, so a circular touching the DBC cannot be excluded.
- AR 308/2021 text not found.

## Which DBC parts beyond Part A matter for modifications, and is the whole code one PDF?

### Takeaway
Yes. One 843-page English PDF (32.6 MB) contains Parts A to K, with no Part I. For an interior wall in an apartment, Parts A, B, C, F, H and possibly G are the main ones, and UAE FLSC remains necessary for fire.

### Cited Findings
- File facts: 843 pages, 32.6 MB, modified 6 December 2021, 1,671 bookmarks. PDF start pages: A 3, B 21, C 151, D 254, E 296, F 327, G 403, H 548, J 657, K 671. [R-direct] — [DBC 2021](https://dmpmedia.dm.gov.ae/uploads/2021/12/Dubai%20Building%20Code_English_2021%20Edition_compressed.pdf)
- Separate DBC tools on the DM page: U-value calculation, glazed schedule, AC unit schedule, villa insulation sections Types 1–6, solar calculator. [R-tool] — [DM DBC page](https://www.dm.gov.ae/municipality-business/planning-and-construction/dubai-building-code-2/)
- A.2: "The DBC applies to renovation, modification, reconstruction, addition to, or change of use of an existing building." Table A.1 sets the reach per change type. [R-direct] — [DBC 2021](https://dmpmedia.dm.gov.ae/uploads/2021/12/Dubai%20Building%20Code_English_2021%20Edition_compressed.pdf)
- A keyword sweep (not a read) found these passages touching existing buildings: [R-direct] — [DBC 2021](https://dmpmedia.dm.gov.ae/uploads/2021/12/Dubai%20Building%20Code_English_2021%20Edition_compressed.pdf)
  - C.5.9.4: lift platforms in existing buildings where a passenger lift cannot be installed.
  - F.7.13.14: seismic assessment of existing buildings follows ASCE 41.
  - Part G telecom section: applies to alterations and refurbishments of existing buildings (PDF p. 508).
  - Part H references BS 8233 and Approved Document E under H.3.1.7. Acoustic terms cluster at PDF pp. 632–635.
  - Part K covers villas and townhouses (A.5.1).
- GFA is measured "from the centrelines of the common walls joining two spaces" (A.2). [R-direct] — [DBC 2021](https://dmpmedia.dm.gov.ae/uploads/2021/12/Dubai%20Building%20Code_English_2021%20Edition_compressed.pdf)

### Inferences
- Likely relevance for modifications, by Part:
  - A: change types, hierarchy, alternative solutions.
  - B: room sizes, sanitary and planning content.
  - C: accessibility routes.
  - D: only with occupant-load or use change.
  - E: only when the envelope changes.
  - F: load-bearing elements.
  - G: services and telecom when systems move.
  - H: ventilation, daylight and acoustics.
  - J: rarely.
  - K: villas.
- This list comes from the Part titles and the sweep, not from reading Parts B to K. It is a plan for indexing, not a finding.
- A moved wall shifts a GFA measuring line by the centreline rule, but total building GFA may not change. A reviewer should confirm this.

### Gaps
- Parts B to K were not read in full. In particular, a numeric sound-insulation requirement between dwellings was not confirmed: the sweep found no "separating wall" term.
- Whether an official Arabic edition of the DBC exists was not established. The DM page gives no Arabic text.

## Local Order 6/2000 and the implementing rules of Local Order 3/1999: location, content, status. Has it been repealed?

### Takeaway
Local Order 3/1999 is still in force, as amended. Local Order 6/2000 only deferred its commencement. The amendment that matters is Executive Council Resolution 65/2022, which replaced art. 4, 8 and 9 and is missing from the source analysis. No single "implementing bylaw" document was found; the rules are spread over several resolutions.

An official English translation exists on DLP, so it is not Arabic-only. ## Regulation 3/2007 and Regulation 10/2006: what are they exactly, issuer, official text, status?

### Takeaway
Both are short Executive Council regulations, issued by the Chairman of the Executive Council, not by DM. They authorise application routes and set fees, and leave substantive criteria to Director General resolutions. Both appear still in force and are read with AR 266/2011.

### Cited Findings
- Regulation 10/2006, "applications to modify planning and construction systems": issued 19 December 2006 by Hamdan bin Mohammed, Chairman of the Executive Council. Gazette 317 of 28 December 2006. In force on issue. Four articles, Arabic. [R-direct] — [PDF](https://dmpmedia.dm.gov.ae/uploads/2024/12/نظام-رقم-10-لسنة-2006-بشأن-طلبات-التعديل-على-أنظمة-التخطيط-والبناء-في-إمارة-دبي.pdf)
- Reg. 10/2006 art. 1: any planning or building parameter in the planning and building legislation at DM may be modified, up or down, horizontally or vertically, under criteria set by Director General resolution. Art. 2 fee: 30% of the land price at application × (area gained by the modification ÷ total permitted built area). Art. 3: no fee for UAE nationals' own homes. [R-direct] — same PDF.
- DLP-style English title differs: "Applications for Exemption from Compliance with Planning and Construction Regulations", as listed in the preamble of Law 16/2023. [R-direct] — [Law 16/2023](https://dlp.dubai.gov.ae/Legislation%20Reference/2023/Law%20No.%20(16)%20of%202023%20Concerning%20Urban%20Planning.pdf)
- Regulation 3/2007, "applications to modify land and building uses": issued 31 December 2007 by the same Chairman. Gazette 332 of 30 June 2008. In force on publication. Five articles, Arabic. [R-direct] — [PDF](https://dmpmedia.dm.gov.ae/uploads/2024/12/نظام-رقم-3-لسنة-2007-بشأن-طلبات-التعديل-على-استعمالات-الأراضي-والمباني-في-إمارة-دبي.pdf); [DLP Arabic HTML](https://dlp.dubai.gov.ae/Legislation%20Ar%20Reference/2008/%D9%86%D8%B8%D8%A7%D9%85%20%D8%A7%D9%84%D9%85%D8%AC%D9%84%D8%B3%20%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%AA%D9%86%D9%81%D9%8A%D8%B0%D9%8A%20%D8%B1%D9%82%D9%85%20(3)%20%D9%84%D8%B3%D9%86%D8%A9%202007.html)
- Reg. 3/2007 art. 1: an application may change the permitted use of land or a building to another use in the land-use classification annexed to Local Order 2/1999, or divide land into plots, subject to criteria set by the Director General. Art. 2 fee: 1% of the land or building value, using the Dubai Land Department estimate. [R-direct] — same PDF.
- AR 266/2011 (issued 22 August 2011): art. 1 defines the change as a change of approved planning use, for example residential to commercial or the reverse, of all or a specific part of the building. Art. 2 gives the fee rules for full and partial changes; a partial change that yields gained area uses the Reg. 10/2006 formula. Art. 3 covers notional-area purchases. [R-direct, scan] — [PDF](https://dmpmedia.dm.gov.ae/uploads/2024/12/قرار-إداري-رقم-266-لسنة-2011-بشأن-طلبات-التعديل-على-أنظمة-التخطيط-والبناء-واستعمالات-الأراضي.pdf)
- Other DM instruments in this chain, from the DM list: AR 66/2007 (rules for amending parameters), AR 115/2020, AR 227/2022, and Executive Council Resolution 5/2017 on fee refunds. [R-tool] — [DM agency list](https://www.dm.gov.ae/municipality-business/buildings-regulation-permits-agency/)
- AR 227/2022 (issued 9 August 2022) applies Reg. 10/2006 fees to its roof-gym conversion item and does not mention apartments or unit mergers [R-tool]. Circular 2-2-1 of 14 November 2022 says it took effect on 24 August 2022 and repeals Circular 3-5-1/2021 [R-direct]. — [AR 227/2022](https://dlp.dubai.gov.ae/Legislation%20Reference/2022/Administrative%20Resolution%20No.%20(227)%20of%202022%20Concerning%20Exemption%20from%20Certain%20Planning.html); [Circular](https://dmpmedia.dm.gov.ae/uploads/2022/11/2-2-1.pdf)
- Status signals: both regulations are named in the preamble of Decree 45/2021 [R-tool] and Law 16/2023 [R-direct]. Law 16/2023 art. 23 repeals only to the extent of conflict and keeps earlier planning resolutions and bylaws in force unless they contradict it. It came into force 90 days after publication. [R-direct] — [Law 16/2023](https://dlp.dubai.gov.ae/Legislation%20Reference/2023/Law%20No.%20(16)%20of%202023%20Concerning%20Urban%20Planning.pdf)

### Inferences
- A wall between two residential apartments does not change the planned use, so Reg. 3/2007 is probably not engaged. Reg. 10/2006 would be engaged only if a planning or building parameter is modified.
- The DBC's "change of use" (occupancy, A.2) and Reg. 3/2007's approved planning use are different tests. They should stay as separate rules: a change can trigger one without the other.
- Both fee formulas are public primary text, which contrasts with the blog figures rightly excluded so far. They could be cited, but only with the AR 266/2011 modifications.

### Gaps
- The DM criteria resolutions were not read (AR 66/2007, AR 115/2020).
- Law 16/2023 was only searched for repeal and citation terms, not read in full. Its effect on the use-change procedure is unverified.
- Later fee changes were not traced.

## Law 14/2025 and Law 7/2025: exact titles, official URLs, relevance to modifications and role competence

### Takeaway
Both are in force, with Arabic official text only found. They set who may design, supervise and build, through registration, classification and competence certificates. Neither sets permit rules for modifications. Law 14 repealed Local Order 89/1994, which DM still lists.

### Cited Findings

## Other relevant law: jointly owned property and a wall between two apartments

### Takeaway
Law 6/2019 is the key missing source. A dividing wall between adjacent units is shared by both owners, and substantial alterations need approval from the Master Developer, DLD and the Competent Authority. This gives the 4A/4B case a primary legal basis for second-owner involvement, but it does not literally state a neighbour NOC.

### Cited Findings
- Law 6/2019 on Ownership of Jointly Owned Real Property: issued 4 September 2019 by the Ruler. In force 60 days after publication (art. 52). Repeals Law 27/2007 (art. 51). Official English translation available; Arabic prevails. Applies across the Emirate, including Special Development Zones and free zones [S]. [R-tool] — [DLP English HTML](https://dlp.dubai.gov.ae/Legislation%20Reference/2019/Law%20No.%20(6)%20of%202019%20Concerning%20Ownership%20of%20Jointly%20Owned%20Real%20Property%20in%20the%20Emirate%20of%20Dubai.html)
- Art. 9(e): "The dividing walls between two (2) adjacent Units will be shared by the Owners of both Units." Art. 9(a) treats non-support internal walls as part of the Unit. [R-tool] — same source.
- Art. 7(a)(1): structural walls, columns and similar are Common Parts. Art. 17: Common Parts may not be converted, subdivided or disposed of, and Common Facilities may not be altered in a restricting way, without prior DLD and Competent Authority approval. [R-tool] — same source.
- Art. 39(a): occupants, including owners, may make substantial alterations to a Unit or the property "only after obtaining the approval of the Master Developer, the DLD, and the Competent Authority." Art. 39(b)–(c): remedy duty and costs. [R-tool] — same source.
- Art. 6: Plans, Master Community Declaration, Statute and Building Management Regulation form part of the title deed. Occupants must comply with them. Art. 13: a jointly owned Unit may be subdivided only if the Declaration allows it and with DLD approval and Competent Authority licences. The law has no express merger provision. [R-tool] — same source.
- Art. 24: the fetch report shows the Owners Committee with oversight and recommendation duties, and no express approval power over alterations. [R-tool] — same source.
- Art. 10 of Law 3/2026 has the Management Entity under Law 6/2019 perform owner duties under that law. [R-tool] — [Law 3/2026](https://dlp.dubai.gov.ae/Legislation%20Reference/2026/Law%20No.%20(3)%20of%202026%20Concerning%20the%20Quality%20and%20Safety%20of%20Buildings.html)
- DLD FAQ, separating or amalgamating a unit: title deed(s), NOC from the developer or the jointly owned property management company, bank NOC if mortgaged, planning-approved plan and the unit's engineering file, parking allocation letter, completion certificate; AED 620 per unit. [R-tool] — [DLD FAQ](https://dubailand.gov.ae/en/frequently-asked-questions/)
- DLD property-data amendment service covers a change in property area, requiring an approved plan and a developer NOC. [S] — [DLD service](https://dubailand.gov.ae/en/eservices/request-to-amend-property-data/)
- DLD FAQ on tenants: if a landlord refuses documents for works needing official permits, the tenant may go to the Rental Disputes Centre, showing the works do not affect the structure. [R-tool] — [DLD FAQ](https://dubailand.gov.ae/en/frequently-asked-questions/)
- AR 85/2019 art. 2(5) and 4(b)(5) require written consent of the owner of the unit for decoration works. It covers commercial-type units only. [R-direct] — [AR 85/2019](https://dmpmedia.dm.gov.ae/uploads/2024/12/قرار-إداري-رقم-85-لسنة-2019-بشأن-ترخيص-أعمال-الديكور.pdf)
- Search found no 2025–2026 amendment of Law 6/2019. [S] — [DLD FAQ](https://dubailand.gov.ae/en/frequently-asked-questions/)

### Inferences
- If the wall dividing 4A and 4B moves, it is the shared wall in art. 9(e), so both owners have a direct interest. Consent of both owners is a sound conservative rule, but it is an interpretation.
- A boundary shift changes both unit areas and therefore likely the title plans, which points to a DLD step after the building permit. The FAQ's amalgamation list is the closest match, but a boundary shift is neither merger nor subdivision, so it needs reviewer confirmation.
- If the wall is structural, it is a Common Part (art. 7) and art. 17 applies. The route is also heavier under AR 109/2022.
- "Substantial alterations" is not defined in what was read, so the threshold remains open.
- The app should add a new rule family alongside the DM permit rules: JOP check, shared-wall check, structural-element check.

### Gaps
- The Arabic text of art. 7, 9, 13, 17 and 39 was not compared. Art. 9 and 39 should be read in Arabic because they carry the case.
- Gazette issue and date for Law 6/2019 not established.
- RERA's standard Building Management Regulation and Statute texts were not found. They may contain a specific alterations clause, and project-level documents will vary.
- The DLD legislation overview page returned HTTP 500 and was not read.
- Whether DM's permit system requires an NOC from the second owner or the management company for apartments is not confirmed by any primary source.

## Terms of reuse, English versus Arabic, and which text is binding

### Takeaway
Arabic prevails wherever the Dubai translation note appears. The DLP terms permit personal or research use, bar republication, and require written consent for commercial use. The DBC's own copyright clause is stricter. RAG use needs a licensing decision before any public demo.

### Cited Findings
- DLP terms: contents are protected by copyright; unless stated otherwise, materials may be downloaded, stored or used for personal or research purposes only. They may not be republished or redistributed, including on other websites. Commercial exploitation needs prior written consent of the Committee. The portal is provided "as is". [R-tool] — [DLP terms](https://dlp.dubai.gov.ae/en/Pages/TermsAndConditions.aspx)
- English translations on DLP state that the Arabic must be consulted for interpretation and that "the Arabic text will prevail" in case of conflict. Seen on LO 3/1999, Decree 45/2021, Law 6/2019 and Law 16/2023. ### Inferences
- Under these terms, a research prototype fits the "research purposes" wording. A demo shown to clients or a commercial product does not clearly fit, and displaying full text in the app risks "making available". Safer design: store locally, show short citations and links, and seek written consent from the Supreme Legislation Committee and Dubai Municipality.
- The DBC clause is broad enough to reach embedding and chunking. Whether it is enforceable against a legal text is a question for a lawyer.
- Index the Arabic text as authoritative and keep English as a retrieval aid, with `authoritative: ar` on each document.

### Gaps
- dm.gov.ae and dubailand.gov.ae terms were not checked.
- Whether UAE copyright law excludes official legislative texts from protection was not researched. This matters and should go to a lawyer.
- Whether the exemption applies to a binding DBC adopted by decree was not established.
